How LawAI Works

LawAI helps officers perform early-stage legal assessment by mapping incident descriptions to relevant legal provisions for FIR readiness.

1. Input Incident Details

Describe incident facts in plain language with as much factual clarity as possible.

2. AI-Driven Legal Mapping

The system evaluates context and identifies potentially applicable legal sections.

3. Structured Legal Output

Results are presented with section references and concise significance notes.

4. FIR Support

Use the output as support material while drafting complete FIR documentation.

This assistant supports professional workflows and does not replace qualified legal judgment.
